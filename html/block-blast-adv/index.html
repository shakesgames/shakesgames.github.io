Blast Blocks!

How to play

  • Drag one of the 3 block pieces onto the 8×8 board (or tap a piece, then tap the board).
  • You score +1 point per block placed, just like the original Block Blast.
  • Fill a full row or column to blast it: cleared blocks score 10 points each, plus bonuses for multi-line clears and combo streaks.
  • New pieces arrive when you use all three. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.
  • Every 500 points earns a 💣 bomb — tap the bomb button up top, then tap any block on the grid to remove it.
